# Story bible

One page that keeps every level in the same world. Read this before writing any level text. Change it first, then the levels.

## The idea in one line

**Kit** is a new courier. Kit delivers one letter across **Packet Valley** and learns how the internet works on the way, then takes the ferry to **Algo Archipelago** to help islanders with their problems.

## Kit (the player character)

- A new courier, about 16. Curious, a little clumsy, never embarrassed to ask "wait, what does that mean?".
- Carries a **satchel**, a **pocket notebook** (where the Field Journal comes from) and a **bicycle bell** that rings when something is explained well.
- Kit asks the questions a beginner would ask. Kit never knows the answer before the player does, and never says "obviously".
- Voice in lessons: friendly, plain, present tense. Short sentences. Talk to "you" and refer to "Kit" for the story.

## Recurring characters

**Packet Valley**
- **Farmer Juno**: the recipient of the letter. Calm, on the far side of the valley. Appears at the end of every zone with a tiny reaction.
- **Baker Bea**: runs the bakery in Town Square. Practical, loves recipes and order.
- **Postmaster Pim**: knows every road. Fussy about addresses.
- **Weaver Wren**: works with thread and wire. Dreamy.
- **Farmer Ollie**: next-door neighbour, always worried a parcel got damaged.
- **Mayor Moss**: wants simple answers and uses the village website all day.

**Algo Archipelago**
- **The lighthouse keeper**: keeps the tool shed, never gives the answer straight away.
- **Captain Ada** and **Captain Bo**: friendly rivals. Ada plans for the long run. Bo shines on small jobs.
- **Painter Pia**, **Ferry Master Fen**, **Tide Clerk Toby**, **Dance Captain Dot**, **Printer Pell**: each needs a different tool.
- **The harbour master**: counts crates, loves a tidy method.

## Places

**Packet Valley** (a district per layer): Town Square (foundations), Wire Woods (physical), Frame Farm (data link), Crowded Plaza (sharing a channel), Router Ranch (network), Harbour of Ports (transport), Market Street (applications), Castle Keep (security), The Grand Delivery (final level).

**Algo Archipelago** (an island per idea): Lighthouse Point (foundations), Brute Bay (brute force), Shrinking Sands (recursion), Bridge Isles (graphs), Split Rock (divide and conquer), Orchard Atoll (heaps and trees), Trade Wind Cove (space and time), Logbook Lagoon (dynamic programming), Far Horizon (coming later), The Grand Expedition (final level).

## Tone

- Cozy and a little funny. Never sarcastic, never scary, never talking down.
- Every new word is explained the first time it appears, by a picture or an everyday comparison (letters, ferries, plates), not by a definition alone.
- Maths: only everyday algebra on the main path. Anything harder lives in "Go deeper".
- Mistakes are normal. Wrong answers get a kind hint, never a joke at the player's expense.
- Security stays conceptual and defensive: why a weakness exists and how it is prevented. No step-by-step attacks.

## Running threads and small jokes

- **The letter for Juno** travels through the whole valley. Every zone moves it one step further.
- **Kit's bicycle bell** rings once when something clicks.
- **Baker Bea's recipe cards** keep blowing away in the wind. Each zone has one more recipe card Kit has to rebuild.
- **Ada and Bo** race something new on every island. Bo wins small races, Ada wins long ones.
- **Mayor Moss** keeps asking for the "simple version" and Kit gives it in one sentence.

## One-line cliffhanger at the end of each zone

**Packet Valley**
- Zone 0, Town Square: the letter's wrapped, but a gust of wind turns it over and the address smudges.
- Zone 1, Wire Woods: the signal arrives, but arrives wrong. Something flipped a bit on the way.
- Zone 2, Frame Farm: the frame is checked and clean, but two couriers started talking at once.
- Zone 3, Crowded Plaza: the plaza is calm again, but the letter's road leads out of the valley.
- Zone 4, Router Ranch: the route is found, but the packet is too big for the next bridge.
- Zone 5, Harbour of Ports: the parcel arrives whole, but Juno's door has no name on it. Where does it go?
- Zone 6, Market Street: the market knows the address now, but a stranger's stamp is on the envelope.
- Zone 7, Castle Keep: the letter is safe, and a ferry whistle sounds from across the water.
- The Grand Delivery: Juno reads the letter. It says: "Come to the islands. They need a courier."

**Algo Archipelago**
- Lighthouse Point: the tool shed is tidy, but the regatta boats are sailing out without a plan.
- Brute Bay: the brute-force way works, but it will take longer than the tide.
- Shrinking Sands: the problem shrank on every step, but a new one is waiting across a bridge.
- Bridge Isles: every island is connected, but the bridges can't all be crossed at once.
- Split Rock: the rock split cleanly in two, and a smell of apples drifts from the orchard.
- Orchard Atoll: the harvest is sorted, but the labels are blowing away in the trade wind.
- Trade Wind Cove: the lookup is instant, but the logbook has never been finished.
- Logbook Lagoon: every sub-answer is saved, and the horizon shows a final challenge.
- The Grand Expedition: Kit looks back at the valley and the islands: same courier, different maps.

## How to use this page

- When you write a level, read the character list and the cliffhanger for its zone.
- Keep characters consistent. Do not invent a new recurring character without adding them here.
- Keep it short. If a detail does not help a level, it does not belong on this page.
